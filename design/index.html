camless

Run your cooler without NZXT CAM

Clickable mockups of camless for one PC: a Kraken Elite V2 with 3 radiator fans and a Control Hub with 4 more. All three look and work like CAM, so moving over takes no relearning. Nothing is sent to hardware, but every page reacts to load, heat and your changes as the real thing would.

camless opens as a small window, as the app will. A bar at the bottom drives the demo: window size, load (idle, game, render), a missing CPU sensor or hot liquid. ‹ and › move to the next layout. On a phone it starts folded: tap ◐.

The simulated cooler behind every layout. Liquid at 28.7 °C, pump at 61 %.

Looks like CAM

CAM 4's dark window, left bar, purple accents and names for everything: AIO Pump, Fan Connector, Temperatures source, Fixed, Silent, Performance and Custom. The three differ only in how the pages are arranged.

    Your fans, as they look

    Every fan channel shows the NZXT fans plugged into it: F RGB Core, F RGB Duo, F RGB or a fan without RGB, in black or white. Click the fan on a cooling card, or Fans under Lighting, to change it.

    What the numbers follow

    The simulation uses camless's real rules: liquid curves are stored on the cooler, CPU and GPU curves are set by camless every second, the safety floor ramps from 45 to 55 °C liquid, and lost sensors fall back to 70 %.
